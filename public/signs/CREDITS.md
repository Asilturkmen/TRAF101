## How the files are made

Every sign is generated by `scripts/signs/build-signs.mjs` from the shared frames and pictograms in `scripts/signs/lib.mjs` and `scripts/signs/signs.mjs`. Run `node scripts/signs/build-signs.mjs` to rebuild them and `node scripts/signs/contact-sheet.mjs <folder>` to render review sheets.

No drawing is traced from, or copied from, another file. No upstream SVG or raster image was used as a base, so every sign has an empty `image.basedOn` list in `content/signs/*.json`.

## Accuracy note

No illustrated KKTC sign catalogue is published online. The set shows the clean, canonical form of signs in use in KKTC, adapted for left-hand traffic and Turkish wording. Real signs on the road may differ in detail. Each sign's evidence status is recorded in `kktc.status` in `content/signs/*.json`.
