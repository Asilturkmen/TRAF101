# KKTC driving-licence and traffic-rule fact base

Research date: 20 October 2026. Scope: Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus (KKTC / TRNC) only.
Purpose: verified source material for the Test2Drive content rewrite. Every fact carries a tag:

- **[V]** verified against an official or primary source: a KKTC law or regulation text (Resmi Gazete / ministry PDF), an official ministry or police page, or an official leaflet.
- **[S]** secondary source only: a local news report, often quoting police, the ministry or the driving-school union, or a driving school.
- **[?]** uncertain, conflicting, or inferred. Must be confirmed before it goes into the question bank.

Source IDs such as (S3) point to the source index at the end of this file.

## TL;DR

1. **The governing law** is the *1974 Motorlu Araçlar ve Yol Trafik Yasası* (21/1974, amended many times) plus the *1974 Motorlu Araçlar ve Yol Trafik Tüzüğü*, the *Yol Kuralları Yasası (Fasıl 334)*, the *Yol Güvenliği Yasası (9/1988)*, the *Emniyet Kemeri Yasası (29/2012)* and the *Yol ve Trafik Suçlarının Davasız Halli ve Ceza Puanı Yasası (43/1995)*. Licences and exams are run by the **Trafik Dairesi** under the **Bayındırlık ve Ulaştırma Bakanlığı (BUB)**. [V] (S2, S6, S7, S8, S11)
2. **Keep left, overtake on the right.** This comes from the Yol Kuralları Yasası, art. 2. [V] (S7) Overtaking a vehicle on its left side is a fineable offence. [S] (S31)
3. **Priority:** at uncontrolled junctions, give way to traffic from the **right**. At a roundabout (*çember*), give way to vehicles coming from the right; a vehicle already in the roundabout has priority. [V] (S1)
4. **Alcohol limits:** 50 mg per 100 ml of blood, 24 **micrograms** per 100 ml of breath, 65 mg per 100 ml of urine. The limit is **0** for public-transport drivers (*kamu taşımacılığı yapan araç şoförleri*). [V] (S8) There is no special lower limit for new drivers in the texts found. [?]
5. **Speed limits:** 50 km/h in built-up areas. Outside built-up areas the limits depend on the road class: 100 km/h for cars on divided roads, 90 on first-class roads, 80 on second-class roads and 70 on village roads. Buses, trucks, motorcycles and towing vehicles have lower limits. [V] for the headline values (S1); [S] for the full table (S27).
6. **Emergency numbers:** **155** Police (*Polis İmdat*), **112** Ambulance / *Acil*, **199** Fire (*İtfaiye*). At an injury accident, call 112 for the ambulance and 155 for the police. **155 is correct for police in KKTC**, so the current site's "155" is incomplete rather than foreign. [V] (S1, S15)
7. **The theory step today is an oral learner-licence exam** (*öğrenci ehliyeti sınavı*), held face to face with a driving-licence inspector (*sürüş ehliyeti müfettişi*) and centred on road signs. A computer-based, multiple-choice exam (50 questions / 40 to pass / 60 minutes for car and motorcycle classes) is written into a 2017/2020 regulation, but it **has not been implemented**. In 2024 and again in December 2025 the authorities decided to move to a written e-exam, with question banks in English, Russian and Arabic (2024) and later "8 languages" (2025). As of April 2026 the oral exam was still in use. [V] for the regulation text (S3); [S] for the practice (S22, S23, S24, S25).
8. **Licence classes in force today are the old KKTC letters, not EU letters.** D = car, H/H1/H2 = motorcycles, J = moped, C = minibus, A = bus, B = heavy goods, E = tractor, F = tracked or heavy machinery, G = road roller, K = other. A 2026 Trafik Dairesi notice still names the "J, H1, H2 veya H" motorcycle classes. [V] (S10, S39) EU-style classes (M, A1, A2, A, B, BE, C1, C, D1, D, F1, F, G) exist only in the unimplemented 2017/2020 regulation. [V] (S4) **"EH" does not exist in either system.**
9. **Foreign licences:** tourists may drive on their own licence for up to **90 days** from entry. Anyone who becomes a resident, worker or **student** loses tourist status and must convert to a KKTC licence. Third-country nationals go through a preparatory traffic-rules test, then a learner licence with at least 5 days of lessons (8 hours of daytime and 2 hours of night driving), then the KKTC licence. [V] (S1, S16) The exact grace period after losing visitor status conflicts between sources. [?]
10. **Penalty points:** 100 points within 15 months means a 3-month licence suspension. Repeat cycles bring 6 months, then 1 year, then 2 years. [V] (S11) Fixed fines are expressed as a percentage of the monthly minimum wage (*asgari ücret*). [V] (S6, S28)

## 1. Theory exam and licensing process

### 1.1 What the law says vs. what happens in practice

There are two layers, and the website must not mix them:

**Status timeline** for the new system:
- **May 2017:** the new regulations are published, with entry into force 24 months later. [V] S3 art. 39; [S] S17
- **28.08.2020:** the regulations are re-published in R.G. 163. [S] S18
- **1.11.2020:** exams and renewals are halted because "the infrastructure was not ready". [S] S18
- **December 2020:** a Council of Ministers decision restarts the old exams. [S] S19
- **2022:** the ministry still plans to "move to the international system for licence categories". [V] S13
- **April 2024:** a decision to make the learner exam written. [V] S14 / [S] S22
- **December 2025:** a decision with Turkey's MEB to move from oral to written e-exams. [S] S23
- **April 2026:** the press still refers to "mandatory oral exams". [S] S24

**Implication for the site:** the 50 / 40 / 60-minute format is the legally designed future format, not the current one. Present it as "the official format set by regulation, being introduced". Do not claim that candidates sit a 50-question computer test today. Practising for it is still valid preparation, because the official topic weights are known.

### 2.3 Classes the current site uses
`src/data/licenses.js` defines **A = Motorcycle, B = Car, C = Truck, D = Bus, EH = Hazard**.
- These are EU letters, but **in current KKTC usage A = bus, B = heavy truck, C = minibus, D = car, H = motorcycle.** A KKTC user reading "B" will think "truck". The site must either use the current letters or label them clearly ("EU-style category B, KKTC class D today").
- **"EH" (hazardous materials) is not a KKTC licence class** in either the old or the new system. Dangerous goods appear only as a vehicle type in the speed-limit notice ("Tehlikeli Madde ve Özel Yük Taşıyan Araçlar") and as an overloading/dangerous-cargo offence. No KKTC ADR-style driver certificate was found. Remove EH, or replace it with an optional "heavy vehicles" track. [V] S3, S4, S10, S27
